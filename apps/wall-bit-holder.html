Privacy Policy

WallBitHolder (wall-bit-holder.meckman.org) · Effective November 8, 2026

What this site is

WallBitHolder is a product website for a wall-mounted holder for 1/4 inch hex bits. It describes the product and explains how to order it by email. The site itself has no forms, accounts or checkout.

What data it accesses

DataWhyHow it is obtained
Nothing, while browsingThe pages are staticThe site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising
Name, email address, shipping address and anything else written in an order emailTo answer questions, take payment and ship the orderSent by the customer by email to orders@meckman.org

Where data is stored

Order emails are forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to the seller's email inbox and kept there. The website stores nothing about visitors.

Who it is shared with

Order details are used only to fulfil the order. A shipping address is given to the shipping carrier to deliver the package. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing. The site's files are served by Cloudflare, which, like any web host, receives standard request information (such as IP address and browser type) when a page loads.

Retention and deletion

Order emails are kept for as long as needed for the order, warranty questions and records. To have your emails deleted, write to orders@meckman.org.

Children

The site is not directed at children and collects nothing from visitors.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
